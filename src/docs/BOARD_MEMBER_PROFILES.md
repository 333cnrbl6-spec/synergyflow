## How the Board Works

### Meeting Structure
1. **Call Meeting:** User or member calls a board meeting with a topic and selects attendees
2. **Present Issue:** Topic is outlined with relevant context
3. **Member Input:** Each board member provides perspective from their expertise
4. **Discussion:** Members can challenge, build on, or refine each other's positions
5. **Vote/Decision:** Members vote or consensus is reached
6. **Action Items:** Decisions are assigned to implementation owners with deadlines
7. **Conclude:** Meeting is recorded with decisions, action items, and next steps

### Decision-Making Framework
- **Unanimous:** Most common—members align on approach
- **Majority:** Used when trade-offs require choice (e.g., timeline vs. quality)
- **Authority:** Some decisions fall within one member's domain (e.g., SynergyFlow on pricing)
- **Escalation:** Rare conflicts escalate to user/founder for final call

### Typical Board Agendas
- Pricing and packaging strategy
- Feature roadmap and prioritization
- Cross-app integrations and API design
- User feedback and product iterations
- Commercial partnerships
- Platform infrastructure and scaling
- Launch timing for new apps
- Customer success initiatives
