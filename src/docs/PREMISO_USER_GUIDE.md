## Dashboard Overview

The Premiso dashboard provides a unified view of all your properties, tenants, and ongoing operations.

### Key Sections
- **Portfolio Summary**: Quick stats on total properties, active tenants, and revenue
- **Pending Tasks**: Maintenance requests, document renewals, and compliance deadlines
- **Quick Actions**: Create new property, add tenant, report maintenance, upload document
- **Recent Activity**: Feed of recent updates across all properties

## Property Management

### Adding a Property
1. Click **New Property** in the dashboard
2. Enter property details:
   - Address and postcode
   - Property type (flat, house, etc.)
   - Number of bedrooms
   - Annual rental value
   - Rent due day (e.g., 1st of month)
3. Optional: Upload property documents (title deeds, EPC, Gas Safety)
4. Click **Save Property**

### Property Details
Each property page shows:
- **Active Tenants**: Current occupants and lease details
- **Documents**: Compliance certificates, title deeds, insurance
- **Maintenance History**: Past issues and work orders
- **Financials**: Rent collected, expenses, net yield

### Bulk Property Actions
- Filter properties by status, location, or tenant type
- Export property data to CSV
- Schedule bulk document renewals

## Tenant Management

### Adding a Tenant
1. Go to a property page
2. Click **Add Tenant**
3. Fill in:
   - Full name, email, phone
   - Start and end dates
   - Monthly rent amount
   - Deposit amount
4. Optional: Create tenancy agreement from template
5. Send invitation email with portal link

### Tenant Portal
Tenants can:
- View rent payment status and history
- Report maintenance issues with photos
- Download important documents
- Receive automated rent reminders

### Rent Payment Tracking
- View all payments (on-time, late, missed)
- Set up payment reminders (3 days before due)
- Generate rent payment reports by tenant or property

## Maintenance Management

### Tenant-Reported Issues
Tenants can report maintenance issues via the portal:
1. Click **Report an Issue**
2. Select category (plumbing, electrical, heating, etc.)
3. Set priority (low, medium, high, emergency)
4. Upload photo evidence
5. Describe the problem in detail

### Tracking Tickets
As a landlord, you can:
- View all open tickets sorted by priority
- Acknowledge issues and update tenants
- Change ticket status (pending → in progress → completed)
- Add internal notes

### Work Orders
For each ticket, generate a work order for contractors:
1. Open a ticket
2. Click **Create Work Order**
3. Assign to contractor
4. Set scheduled dates and estimated cost
5. Add access instructions
6. Send to contractor (email or SMS)

### Contractor Portal
Contractors can:
- View assigned work orders
- Upload completion photos
- Submit invoices
- Update work status

## Automated Messaging

### Email Templates
Premiso includes pre-built templates for:
- **Rent Reminders**: 3 days before rent is due
- **Late Payment Notices**: 3, 7, and 14 days after due date
- **Maintenance Updates**: Notification of scheduled work
- **Tenancy Renewals**: 90 and 30 days before lease ends
- **Document Expiration**: Alerts for upcoming certificate renewals

### Customizing Templates
1. Go to **Settings > Email Templates**
2. Click **Edit** on a template
3. Modify subject and body
4. Use placeholders: {{tenant_name}}, {{property_name}}, {{rent_amount}}, etc.
5. Preview and save

### Automation Rules
Set up rules to automatically send emails:
- **On schedule**: Send rent reminders every month on a specific date
- **On event**: Send notification when tenant reports maintenance
- **On condition**: Send overdue notice only to tenants 7+ days late

### Email Log
View all sent emails:
- Date sent, recipient, template used
- Status (delivered, bounced, failed)
- Click to view full content

## Document Management

### Uploading Documents
1. Go to **Property > Documents**
2. Click **Upload Document**
3. Select document type:
   - Gas Safety Certificate
   - EPC (Energy Performance Certificate)
   - EICR (Electrical Installation Condition Report)
   - Insurance
   - Title Deeds
   - Tenancy Agreement
   - Other
4. Upload file (PDF, JPG, PNG, max 10MB)
5. Enter expiration date (optional)
6. Save

### Document Status Tracking
- **Valid**: Certificate is current
- **Expiring Soon**: Renewal alert triggered 30 days before expiry
- **Expired**: Overdue renewal required
- **Archived**: Old documents kept for records

### Automated Renewal Alerts
Premiso sends reminders when:
- Gas Safety: 30 days before expiry
- EPC: 30 days before expiry
- EICR: 30 days before expiry
- Insurance: 14 days before expiry

### Document Templates
For legal documents, use smart templates with auto-population:
- **Tenancy Agreements**: Auto-fill tenant, property, rent details
- **Notice to Quit**: Auto-calculate notice period
- **Rent Demand Letter**: Auto-populate outstanding amount
- **Maintenance Notice**: Auto-schedule inspection date

## Compliance & Deadlines

### Compliance Checklist
Key requirements for UK landlords:
- ✅ Gas Safety Certificate (annual)
- ✅ EICR electrical report (5-yearly)
- ✅ EPC (before letting, renewable)
- ✅ Tenancy deposit protection (within 30 days)
- ✅ Right to Rent checks
- ✅ Furnishings declarations

### Deadline Tracking
All deadlines appear in your dashboard:
- **Red flags**: Overdue items requiring immediate action
- **Yellow flags**: Due within 7 days
- **Blue flags**: Upcoming (30+ days)

Each deadline shows:
- What's due
- Why it's required (regulation/reference)
- Date due
- Action required

### Compliance Reports
Generate quarterly reports showing:
- Status of all compliance items
- Documents uploaded and verified
- Tenants with active issues
- Recommended actions

## Reports & Analytics

### Available Reports
1. **Portfolio Summary**: Properties, tenants, revenue overview
2. **Rental Income**: By property, by month, by tenant
3. **Maintenance History**: Issues reported, costs, average resolution time
4. **Compliance Status**: All certificates, expiration dates, renewals
5. **Tenant Report**: Payment history, issues reported, tenure

### Custom Reports
Create custom reports with:
- Date range selection
- Property/tenant filtering
- Metrics selection (revenue, maintenance, compliance, etc.)
- Export to PDF or CSV

### Analytics Dashboard
Visual insights:
- **Rent Payment Rate**: % on-time, late, overdue
- **Maintenance Costs**: By category, by property, trends
- **Tenant Lifetime Value**: Revenue per tenant
- **Yield Analysis**: ROI by property

## Troubleshooting

### Emails Not Sending
- Check tenant email address is correct
- Verify email template is active (not archived)
- Check spam folder

### Document Upload Failed
- Ensure file is under 10MB
- File must be PDF, JPG, or PNG
- Try re-uploading with different file

### Maintenance Not Visible to Tenant
- Confirm tenant email verified in portal
- Check ticket status is not "cancelled"
- Verify tenant has property access in system
