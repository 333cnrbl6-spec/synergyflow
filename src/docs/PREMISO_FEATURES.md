# Premiso – Complete Feature Set

## Overview
Premiso is a comprehensive property management platform designed for UK landlords. It automates rent collection, maintenance management, compliance tracking, and tenant communications.

---

## Core Features

### 1. Property Portfolio Management
- **Unlimited properties**: Manage single let or portfolio
- **Property profiles**: Address, type, size, specifications
- **Document storage**: Upload deeds, certificates, insurance
- **Multi-property view**: Dashboard across all properties at once
- **Property analytics**: Yield, ROI, expense tracking by property

### 2. Tenant Management
- **Tenant profiles**: Contact, lease dates, payment history
- **Smart notifications**: Automated email/SMS to tenants
- **Tenant portal**: View payments, report issues, access documents
- **Right to Rent checks**: Compliance validation for each tenant
- **Bulk tenant operations**: Add, invite, or offboard in bulk

### 3. Automated Rent Reminders
- **Pre-rent reminders**: 3 days before rent due
- **Late payment follow-ups**: Automatic emails at 3, 7, 14 days late
- **Customizable templates**: Modify tone and content
- **Smart scheduling**: Send at specific times (e.g., 9am)
- **Email tracking**: See open rates, delivery status

### 4. Maintenance Ticketing
- **Tenant-initiated tickets**: Report issues with photos
- **Priority levels**: Emergency, high, medium, low
- **Smart categorization**: Plumbing, electrical, heating, appliance, structural, etc.
- **Status tracking**: Pending → acknowledged → in progress → completed
- **SLA monitoring**: Alerts when tickets exceed target resolution time
- **Work order generation**: Create contractor work orders from tickets

### 5. Contractor Management
- **Work order system**: Issue detailed instructions to contractors
- **Cost tracking**: Estimated vs. actual costs
- **Materials lists**: Track parts and labor separately
- **Photo evidence**: Contractors upload before/after photos
- **Invoice management**: Generate and track invoices
- **Payment status**: Track whether invoices are paid

### 6. Document Management
- **Smart categorization**: Gas Safety, EPC, EICR, insurance, deeds, tenancy agreements
- **Expiration tracking**: Automatic alerts when certificates expiring
- **Renewal reminders**: 30 days, 14 days before expiry
- **File storage**: Secure PDF/image upload (max 10MB per file)
- **Bulk document actions**: Download, delete, or renew in batch
- **Audit trail**: See who uploaded what and when

### 7. Template System
- **Pre-built templates**: Tenancy agreements, notice to quit, rent demand letters
- **Smart placeholders**: Auto-populate {{tenant_name}}, {{property_address}}, {{rent_amount}}
- **Custom templates**: Create your own legal documents
- **Preview before send**: Test how documents look with real data
- **Batch generation**: Generate multiple documents at once

### 8. Compliance & Deadlines
- **Automated deadline tracking**: All UK landlord obligations tracked
- **Compliance checklist**: Gas Safety, EICR, EPC, deposit protection, Right to Rent
- **Deadline alerts**: Email/SMS when compliance action needed
- **Regulatory reference**: Why each requirement matters and legal basis
- **Compliance reports**: Generate quarterly certification of compliance

### 9. Financial Tracking
- **Rent payment records**: All payments logged with dates
- **Late payment tracking**: Days overdue, total outstanding
- **Expense logging**: Maintenance, repairs, insurance costs
- **Income vs. expense**: Monthly P&L by property
- **Yield analysis**: ROI and cap rate calculations
- **Tax-ready exports**: Download for accountant

### 10. Analytics & Reporting
- **Portfolio dashboard**: Total income, active tenants, pending maintenance
- **Payment analytics**: On-time %, late %, overdue trends
- **Maintenance metrics**: Average resolution time, cost per category, trends
- **Compliance status**: Summary of all certificates, expiration dates
- **Tenant lifetime value**: Revenue, maintenance cost, tenure by tenant
- **Custom reports**: Build reports with filters and export

## Data & Privacy

### Data Storage
- **Location**: UK data centers (AWS London region)
- **Backup**: Daily automated backups with 30-day retention
- **Retention**: Data retained until account deletion (1-year grace period)
- **Compliance**: GDPR compliant, Right to erasure supported

### Security
- **Encryption**: AES-256 for data at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit
- **Access control**: 2FA available, role-based permissions
- **Audit logging**: All actions logged with user, timestamp, changes
- **Compliance**: SOC 2 Type II certified, PCI DSS scope excluded
- **Penetration testing**: Annual third-party security audits

### Privacy
- **Data use**: Your data never trains our AI or used for marketing
- **No selling**: We never sell or share tenant/property data
- **GDPR**: Full data subject rights supported (access, portability, deletion)
- **Cookies**: Minimal analytics cookies, no tracking pixels
- **Terms**: Clear, plain English terms of service
